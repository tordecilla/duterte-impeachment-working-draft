The earlier House submission

The AMLC transmittal is dated April 22, 2026. It responds to a House Committee on Justice subpoena requesting transaction reports for 2006–2025. Its accompanying table reports a combined transaction value of PHP 6,771,227,712.95.

During the October 5 hearing, the parties and witness discussed the earlier House figures alongside changes in coverage and corrections to 13 covered-transaction reports. This collection preserves that older submission separately from the July 30 and October 1 court submissions.
