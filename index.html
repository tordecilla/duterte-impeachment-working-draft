About the project

A clearer way to track a long, consequential public proceeding.

This project turns hours of Senate video into material that can be searched, read, heard, and checked against the original source. It is intended for journalists reporting on deadline, researchers tracing testimony and rulings, students studying the proceeding, and citizens who want to follow the trial closely.

The workflow combines AI-powered transcription with agentic AI review of speaker identities, legal language, fragmented turns, and caption gaps. The reviewed transcript then supports AI-generated session digests and two-voice audio overviews, each linked back to the relevant video timestamps.

Official Senate video→AI transcription→Agentic review→Digest and audio overview
Always verify

AI can mishear, misidentify, omit, or overstate. Use this project to navigate the record, then confirm consequential details against the official Senate video before quoting or publishing them.

Working Draft is an independent, AI-assisted reference for the Impeachment Trial of Vice President Sara Duterte. It is not an official Senate record.
