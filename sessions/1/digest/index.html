Witnesses and evidence

Cylene May L. Del Campo, a Commission on Audit state auditor and prosecution witness, described post-audit work by the Commission on Audit and its Intelligence and Confidential Funds Audit Office (ICFAO), including audit observation memoranda (AOMs), notices of suspension or disallowance, acknowledgement receipts (ARs), responses, and supporting records.

00:37:19 · S0010400:39:37 · S00110

For the Office of the Vice President, Cylene May L. Del Campo testified that an earlier Commission on Audit notice of disallowance totaled ₱73.287 million: ₱69.787 million in reward payments and ₱3.5 million for tables, chairs, desktop computers, and printers. She also testified that one amount contained a typographical error, that the notice was affirmed on petition for review, and that a subsequent motion for reconsideration remained pending. These matters are reported as testimony, not independently verified fact.
