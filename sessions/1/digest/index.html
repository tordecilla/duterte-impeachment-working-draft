Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Juan Miguel "Migz" F. Zubiri proposed moving Senate sessions to 10 a.m. and impeachment-court proceedings to 3 p.m. so evidence and cross-examination could continue into the evening without interruption, while preserving both legislative work and public access. He sought to place the proposal as a motion and then asked for a decision after caucus and the minority leader's participation, stressing that parties and senator-judges needed advance notice rather than a surprise schedule change. The Chair treated it as a possible adjustment, not an immediate order: any shift would require notice to the parties and would be decided after caucus.
