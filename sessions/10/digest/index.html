At a glance

  • Bottom line: The court completed Atty. Jeremy C. Lotoc's testimony after continued defense cross-examination, prosecution redirect, and defense recross.
  • Evidence check: Lotoc said the recordings established that Vice President Sara Duterte uttered the cited statements, but he lacked personal knowledge of the underlying circumstances and the Cybercrime Division had no other evidence identifying the unnamed person.
