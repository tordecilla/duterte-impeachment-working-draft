Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Pia S. Cayetano withdrew her motion in the interest of fairness, saying she was amenable to allowing the prosecution the same latitude for a closing-like statement. She said rules were necessary to keep the proceeding orderly and that senator-judges should call the Chair's attention to possible violations when justice required it.

00:14:45 · S00047

Sen. Pia S. Cayetano asked the Secretariat to install a monitor for the front-row senator-judges, saying the existing viewing position caused neck strain during videos and made affidavits difficult to read.

01:15:21 · S00581

Sen. Paolo Benigno "Bam" Aquino asked John Mark Sta. Ana Calilung to explain the seven deficiencies that led the DOJ to return the first affidavit. Calilung said the first six were technical or evidentiary defects that the NBI addressed; on the final question of whether the NBI was the offended party in a grave-threat case, he said former NBI Director Jaime Santiago's letter explained that a victim's complaint affidavit was not required at preliminary investigation. Calilung said the DOJ then certified that the evidence was sufficient to proceed. Aquino also established that Calilung had investigated the videos, not the alleged hitman, and reserved further questions for a witness on that subject.

01:45:34 · S0087801:48:43 · S0089201:49:46 · S00899

Sen. Joel Villanueva asked how the government assesses a reported threat against the President and what weight its security response should carry in evaluating the allegation. John Mark Sta. Ana Calilung said he could not speak for the National Security Council or other institutions, but that as an NBI agent he considered whether a threat was active, the speaker's capacity to carry it out, and the manner of delivery, including whether it was presented as serious rather than a joke. Villanueva tested whether public office alone supplied that capability; Calilung said it was not the only consideration. Villanueva then clarified that the affidavit submitted to the House was the February version that had complied with DOJ requirements, not the January affidavit first submitted to the DOJ.

01:58:15 · S0096102:01:01 · S00979

Senate President Sherwin T. Gatchalian asked why the NBI subpoenaed six of about 40 people who attended the November 23 press conference. Calilung said investigators selected people they could confirm were present, especially those whose faces and names appeared because they asked questions; all six said they had attended and heard the Vice President's remarks. Gatchalian then examined Calilung's two-week training through MaxCorp Interventures. Calilung said it was bundled with the procurement of digital-forensics tools and covered both their use and the broader process; he distinguished the equipment supplier from the trainer it provided, saying the trainer had digital-forensics certifications but he could not recall them beyond CompTIA and ethical-hacking credentials. Asked about his own qualifications, Calilung distinguished the MaxCorp certificate of training from his certification as a computer-hacking forensic investigator. When Gatchalian asked whether he held a certification specifically for forensic examination of video and similar material, Calilung's answer was incomplete in the record and did not identify a distinct video-forensics credential.

02:01:27 · S0098402:02:39 · S0099802:03:09 · S0100002:03:28 · S0100202:04:17 · S0100602:05:04 · S0101002:05:37 · S0101802:06:23 · S01026

Erwin Tulfo asked whether the NBI's statutory authority to investigate threats to the President, Vice President, Senate President, Speaker, and Chief Justice, particularly those implicating national security, also allowed it to file a case without a request from the threatened official. The Chair said the Court held the documents pre-marked at pretrial, while documents used only in cross-examination had not yet been formally offered; the Clerk would provide what it had, subject to later admissibility rulings. Marcos reiterated that the materials should be adequately identified and assembled promptly.

Calilung said DOJ required his affidavit as the screen recorder, that he had recorded a publicly available video, and that the NBI had not obtained the original file.

02:28:29 · S0115402:29:57 · S0116802:31:21 · S01182

Calilung said he compared the screen recording with the original and found identical contents; Lacson said original media and forensic analysis were important safeguards, particularly given AI.
