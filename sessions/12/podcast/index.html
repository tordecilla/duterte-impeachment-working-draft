02

The big picture

The July 8 session concluded the cross-examination and senator-judge questioning of National Bureau of Investigation Senior Agent John Mark Calilung. The record focused on revisions to the bureau's referral, the source and authentication of the recorded video, the scope of Calilung's investigation, and the context of the statements under review. Defense counsel read a February Department of Justice certification that had recommended further case buildup. Calilung testified that a later certification found the revised submission sufficient for preliminary investigation. He said his authentication relied on O-B-S screen-recording software and manual comparison, rather than an original-source recording or a forensic-tool analysis of this video. In its allotted response, the defense argued that the prosecution had not proved that Vice President Sara Duterte contracted an assassin, and that the statements did not constitute an impeachable high crime. The court allowed broad cross-examination, left admissibility questions for formal offer, and ordered written submissions and later oral argument on pending subpoena requests involving a sealed box and bank records. The court adjourned to July 13, when the prosecution said Jeremy Lotoc would be its next witness.
