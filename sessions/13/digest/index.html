Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Panfilo M. Lacson asked whether the Presiding Officer's previous statement that conviction requires 16 votes, coupled with its reference to judicial review, was a personal legal opinion or a ruling of the Impeachment Court. If it was a ruling, he asked whether it created a justiciable controversy the Supreme Court could resolve should either panel challenge it as grave abuse of discretion.

00:08:16 · S00024

Sen. Francis "Kiko" Pangilinan clarified that the Chair's stated figure of 16 concerned only the two-thirds threshold for conviction. He said the separate question whether Senators Rodante Marcoleta and Jinggoy Estrada should participate in the trial could be discussed later in camera or on the floor. He later asked John Mark Sta. Ana Calilung whether his computer-forensics training extended beyond the initial two weeks. Calilung said it continued through on-the-job training by his chief; he said the initial training was held at the NBI office and handled by MaxCore Interventures, the provider of the bureau's forensic software.

00:15:09 · S0003702:03:20 · S0064202:03:58 · S00650

Sen. Alan Peter Cayetano maintained a continuing objection to the Chair's 16-vote ruling, arguing that Senators Rodante Marcoleta and Jinggoy Estrada should participate because excluding a still-serving senator from voting would effectively count toward acquittal. If the prosecution sought a subpoena for Vice President Duterte, he proposed advance memoranda so senator-judges could study the impeachment-specific self-incrimination issue before oral argument. He also urged the Court to view the complete November 23 recording, or an agreed contextual portion, before selected clips. On authentication, he asked how Calilung could verify that a screen-recorded copy was the same as the Facebook original when their hashes differed; Calilung said the hash covered only the recorded file and that he manually compared the videos. Cayetano later objected that the prosecution had to let Calilung describe the video before asking about particular alleged threats, rather than use a leading question without that foundation.

Prosecution position

00:28:19 · S00078

The prosecution offered Calilung to establish his digital-forensics background, his role as agent on case, and his collection, preservation, and authentication of the November 23 recording and related digital material.

00:36:43 · S00101

On the selected-video dispute, prosecution counsel argued that Rule 132 allowed it to present the portions it considered relevant and left the defense free to inquire into or present the remainder.

01:37:45 · S00524

Responding to Risa Hontiveros, prosecution counsel acknowledged that the statements did not by themselves prove with certainty that Duterte actually contracted an assassin, but argued that the October and November statements showed an asserted plan, intent, knowledge, or system and supported the charged betrayal of public trust.
