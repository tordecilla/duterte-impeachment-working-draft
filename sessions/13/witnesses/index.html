John Mark Sta. Ana Calilung

National Bureau of Investigation Senior Agent; prosecution witness

Calilung identified himself as a 31-year-old NBI senior agent and testified that his training included computer and mobile forensics and photo-and-video authentication.

He testified that the NBI asked Meta to preserve the November 23 livestream, that he screen-recorded the source with OBS, manually compared the output with the source, and calculated a hash for the recorded output.
