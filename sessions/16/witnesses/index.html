01

Gina F. Acosta

Office of the Vice President employee; prosecution witness

Over the defense’s objection, the court declared Acosta a hostile witness under Rule 132, Section 13. The ruling permitted leading, but not misleading, questions during the prosecution’s direct examination.

00:59:56 · S0022501:00:36 · S0023701:01:08 · S00239

Acosta testified that she released ₱125 million to Colonel Raymond Dante Lachica after an instruction she identified as coming from Vice President Sara Duterte. This was her testimony, not a finding by the court.

03:42:05 · S0116103:43:11 · S01164

She also testified that, after the release, Lachica gave her utilization reports and would liquidate funds after confidential activities or operations were implemented. This too remained witness testimony rather than a court finding.

03:53:30 · S0122303:54:16 · S01229

Defense challenged AI-generated imagery used to compare cash bags, questioning its source and foundation. The chair directed Acosta to answer and later recorded a continuing objection to the representations.

Marivic M. Pareja

House archives official; prosecution witness

The prosecution offered Pareja to establish her House Archives position and custody of listed records. Defense then stipulated to her position, custody, and the documents’ transmittal and official-record status.

00:13:12 · S0004400:15:01 · S0005000:18:31 · S00065

The court’s judicial notice was limited to the documents’ existence, submission to the House, and status as official House records—not the truth or relevance of their contents.
