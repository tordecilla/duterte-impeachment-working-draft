At a glance

  • Bottom line: Army officers Manaros M. Boransing II and Magtangol G. Panopio testified that their certifications documented 2023 youth and information activities, not DepEd's confidential-fund spending.
  • What Boransing said: Boransing said the 1st Infantry Division received no DepEd funding for its 2023 YLS and IEC activities and that he did not know his certification would be used in DepEd's COA response.
  • The dispute: The prosecution said DepEd used Army certifications to support reported reward payments, while the defense pointed to their references to DepEd collaboration and information.
  • What the court did: The court accepted the parties' stipulation to identified documents, corrected the prosecution's description of an expenditure as disallowed, and clarified that the burden of proof remained with the prosecution.
  • What's next: The prosecution said Atty. Michael Wesley Poa would testify first on September 2, with a PSA representative to follow if time permitted.

Prosecution position

The prosecution offered Boransing's testimony to show that the 1st Infantry Division conducted its 2023 YLS and IEC activities without DepEd confidential funding and that his certification did not identify information sources, informer payments or a link to DepEd confidential funds.

00:17:56 · S0006000:21:11 · S00071
