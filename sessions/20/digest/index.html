Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Vicente C. Sotto III placed on the record his view that prosecution counsel had generally been allowed to answer in the prior sitting, and identified Republic Act No. 6085 in explaining the distinction he drew between an alias attached to a person and a fictitious name.

00:08:26 · S0001000:08:38 · S0001200:08:49 · S00017

Sen. Erwin Tulfo asked Col. Manaros Boransing about funding for the 2023 Youth Leadership Summits and Information Education Campaigns, the request for the certification, and whether the division had received DepEd rewards or incentives. Boransing said the division had received no DepEd funds in 2023, that Undersecretary Mempin requested the certification through the chain of command, that he did not know it would be used in confidential-fund liquidation, and that similar certifications had been sought from other Army CMO units. In a later round with Col. Magtangol Panopio, Tulfo asked whether the programs were community-relations activities or intelligence gathering; Panopio limited his knowledge to civil-military operations and said he could not address intelligence-unit activity or the distinction between confidential and intelligence funds in the context at issue.

Sen. Risa Hontiveros examined Boransing on the limits of his division's knowledge and certification. Sen. Joel Villanueva asked Boransing what purpose had been given for the requested certification and what it covered. Boransing said no purpose was stated, described the certificate as recording activities and participating agencies, and said intelligence work could inform preparation and target selection but was not conducted during the Youth Leadership Summit or Information Education Campaign itself; he did not claim expertise on intelligence funding or proof that an informer's information produced a particular result. Villanueva then asked Panopio about his own certification and duties. Panopio said no specific purpose was given, his certificate concerned the activities, and he did not know it would form part of a COA liquidation report; he said his certification had no relation to the reported PHP 15.54 million in rewards to informers.

Senate President Sherwin T. Gatchalian asked Boransing whether DepEd supplied confidential or surveillance information for the Youth Leadership Summits and Information Education Campaigns. Boransing said none was supplied to the 1st Infantry Division, described DepEd's role as coordination around the youth participants, and said AFP intelligence was used in choosing locations. Gatchalian later tested the language of Panopio's certification: Panopio described information from DepEd as community conditions and, as an example of confidential material, personal circumstances of students; he said locations were selected through planning and coordination with local governments and other agencies.

Tulfo asked about the reported PHP 15.54 million in rewards, including whether recipients might have been Army operatives, whether DepEd's input to Youth Leadership Summits was confidential, and whether the Army received DepEd confidential funds. Sen. Panfilo "Ping" M. Lacson examined Boransing's ability to know whether DepEd funds had reached the 1st Infantry Division and the scope of his certification.
