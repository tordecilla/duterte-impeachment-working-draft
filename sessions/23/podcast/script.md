## Introduction

This is Working Draft's audio overview of the Impeachment Trial of Vice President Sara Zimmerman Duterte for September 8, 2026. This is a working reference, not an official Senate record.

## A possible future request

Attorney Lorna Patajo-Kapunan said the prosecution reserved a possible future request, after all four articles, to call Vice President Sara Zimmerman Duterte. She also mentioned a possible amicus curiae, a legal expert invited to advise the court, on impeachment and public accountability. No subpoena request was before the court.

## Article 1 stays open

But Article 1 did not close. Presiding Officer Senator Francis "Chiz" Escudero directed both sides not to rest it while the court considers whether to call additional witnesses. If it does, each side may cross-examine and offer rebuttal or surrebuttal evidence about those witnesses.

## The threshold question

Senator-Judge Erwin Tulfo asked how the constitutional reference to all members applies to a final vote when senators are unavailable. The chair treated the proposal as an appeal or reconsideration of the chair's existing requirement of 16 votes to convict: two-thirds of the Senate's 24 members.

## Competing views

Senator-Judge Bam Aquino described the dispute as one of constitutional interpretation, separate from guilt or innocence. Senator-Judge Joel Villanueva supported time to study it and asked how the court would vote on the appeal, distinguishing that vote from the two-thirds requirement for conviction.

## Questions about reopening it

Senator-Judge Pia Cayetano questioned whether the Impeachment Court could reopen the chair's constitutional interpretation. She also asked that any invited experts include differing views. Senator-Judge Robin Padilla urged adherence to the Constitution and said change should follow the amendment process.
