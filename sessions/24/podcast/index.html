01

Introduction

This is the September 14, 2026 audio overview of the impeachment trial of Vice President Sara Duterte. The court opened Article 2 with the sworn testimony of retired Sandiganbayan Presiding Justice Amparo M. Cabotaje-Tang.

02

The day's record

Justice Tang gave legal-framework testimony about unexplained wealth, SALN disclosure, and business-interest restrictions for senior executive officials. The defense objected to her appearance and cross-examined her about the limits of that testimony. Presiding Officer Francis Chiz Escudero allowed the examination subject to cross-examination, then set the next witnesses and bank-document deadlines.

What Article 2 covers

Prosecutor Gerville Luistro described Article 2 as covering unexplained wealth, SALN violations, and non-divestment of financial and business interests. The prosecution offered Justice Tang as a framework witness after she took the oath.

04

Tang's framework testimony

Tang distinguished hidden wealth, unexplained wealth, and ill-gotten wealth. She said a rebuttable presumption may arise when a public officer acquires property during incumbency that is grossly disproportionate to salary, lawful income, or legitimately acquired sources. She also said an omission from a SALN alone does not establish unexplained wealth because the officer must have an opportunity to explain it.

05

The defense objection and the court's direction

Defense counsel Mark Vinluan called Tang an unlisted surprise witness and said the defense lacked the materials it needed to prepare. Senator-Judge Raffy Tulfo proposed hearing first-hand witnesses first. Escudero allowed Tang's testimony, subject to cross-examination, while recording the defense's continuing objection.

A direct exchange on the stand

During cross-examination, Vinluan asked Tang whether she had been promised anything in exchange for testifying. She first said she would never testify if offered anything material or otherwise, then replied: My testimony is not for sale, sir.

07

The limits of the testimony

Tang said she had made no conclusion of fact or law about this case; she had discussed anti-corruption laws and Supreme Court applications. When Vinluan asked whether she had personal knowledge that Vice President Duterte had amassed unexplained wealth, she answered, No, sir.

08

Questions from senator-judges

Senator-Judge Risa Hontiveros asked about the requirements for the prima-facie presumption, and Tang listed public-officer status, acquisition during incumbency, and property grossly disproportionate to lawful income. Senator-Judge Francis Pangilinan asked about judicial notice in the impeachment court; Tang agreed with the propositions he put to her.

Next session and document deadlines

After excusing Justice Tang, the court partially granted PNB and Union Bank more time to produce subpoenaed documents. The remaining documents are due September 17, on a non-extendable deadline.
