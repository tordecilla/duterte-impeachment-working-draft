# Audio overview — September 15, 2026

A concise, source-linked English overview of the Senate impeachment court's September 15 proceedings.

## Introduction

This is Working Draft's audio overview of the Impeachment Trial of Vice President Sara Duterte for September 15, 2026. This is a working reference, not an official Senate record. The Senate impeachment court heard Atty. Karen S. Batu, Officer-in-Charge of the Office of the Ombudsman's Central Records Division. She testified as a records custodian about Vice President Sara Zimmerman Duterte's Statements of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Worth, or SALN, records.

## Scope of the records

The defense objected to using records from before Duterte became Vice President. The chair allowed those records only to establish a baseline, said they would not add pre-vice-presidential charges, and recorded the defense's continuing objection.

## What Batu addressed

Batu said her office prepared and submitted the SALN records supplied to the House. The chair limited her testimony to the official records and the contents subscribed to by the declarant, rather than the truth of the underlying entries. The defense put on the record that the copies being identified were redacted.

## Declared entries and limits

Batu read a declared 2007 net-worth figure of 7,250,497 pesos and said the figures she read were amounts declared in SALNs submitted to the Ombudsman. Asked whether missing acquisition cost could mean net worth was undervalued, she said she could not make that inference because the Ombudsman is a repository agency.

## Close of the session

After the examination, the chair excused Batu. The court directed Attorney Gerardo Del Rosario of the SEC to appear on September 21 and 22, directed distribution of memoranda for the next day's amici curiae session, and adjourned until September 16 at 10 a.m.
