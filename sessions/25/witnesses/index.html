Atty. Karen S. Batu

Officer-in-Charge, Central Records Division, Office of the Ombudsman; records custodian

Batu identified herself as the Officer-in-Charge of the Central Records Division. She described that division as handling case records, including Statements of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Worth (SALNs).

00:23:30 · S0006000:25:05 · S0008000:25:28 · S00081

She said the office prepared and submitted the SALN records to the House. The defense stated that the copies then being identified were redacted.

00:28:07 · S0011900:29:43 · S00129BB00:31:27 · S00144

Batu described the Ombudsman as a repository agency. She said she could not infer from a missing acquisition-cost entry that declared net worth was undervalued, and later said her testimony was limited to what was written in the submitted SALNs.

01:40:11 · S0055702:03:35 · S0068803:24:36 · S01048
