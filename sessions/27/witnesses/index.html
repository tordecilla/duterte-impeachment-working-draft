Atty. Gerardo F. del Rosario

SEC Company Registration and Monitoring Department Director

The prosecution called Atty. Gerardo F. del Rosario as its third Article II witness. Del Rosario identified himself as the SEC's Company Registration and Monitoring Department director, a position he said he had held since 2018.

00:14:31 · S00047BB00:22:57 · S0007100:23:53 · S00075

Del Rosario described the SEC office that keeps corporate filings and records. He said the department located, printed and certified requested corporate documents before they were transmitted to the House of Representatives.

On Metro City Chow Food Corporation, Del Rosario testified that the SEC filings listed Vice President Sara Duterte as an incorporator and director, with a 20-percent subscription, and later as a board and executive-compensation-committee member.

He also said his department had no access to the beneficial-ownership forms discussed in the questioning.
