Atty. Roderick C. Wamil

Commission on Audit team leader; prosecution witness

Wamil described a compliance audit based on documents submitted to COA's Intelligence and Confidential Fund Audit Office and Joint Circular 2015-01, rather than firsthand observation of confidential operations or recipient transactions.
