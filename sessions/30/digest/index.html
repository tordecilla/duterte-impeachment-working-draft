Witnesses and evidence

PhilGEPS official Rendell P. Sopeña testified from an extract described in questioning as 49 GenCorp awards. He confirmed 15 entries awarded by Davao City and 33 by PhilHealth Region XI. When counsel described the remaining entry as awarded by OWWA Region XI, Sopeña corrected that it was awarded to OWWA by a procuring entity. He explained that procuring entities upload the award data and documents, while his office serves as the repository and does not oversee actual bidding or compliance.

Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Francis "Chiz" G. Escudero questioned PhilGEPS official Rendell Sopeña about the limits of his knowledge of uploaded procurement documents. Sopeña confirmed that procuring entities upload them, that he did not prepare them, and that PhilGEPS is a repository rather than an oversight or compliance office. Asked whether his office detects defects or alerts the uploading entity, he said it does neither and suggested irregularities are generally found in an audit. Escudero also observed that the Vice President's SALN described her as a GenCorp shareholder while an SEC witness had said the company's submitted records did not list her; he said the discrepancy remained to be reconciled. During Police Major Joericson Sangalang's testimony, Escudero asked about the firearms list and whether the defense would stipulate to it. Defense counsel said it had not received the list and disputed the witness's relevance. Escudero questioned the probative value of the firearms' estimated value against the Vice President's declared assets and suggested firearms need not be separately listed in a SALN; the prosecution cited guidelines it said required individual declaration, while the defense later disputed their applicability. Sangalang said his values came from a current market survey, not known acquisition prices, and that he could check records about the mode of transfer. At Escudero's request, he gave separate survey totals of ₱2,184,550 for the Vice President and ₱5,035,820 for her spouse; Escudero stressed these were not necessarily acquisition costs. When counsel raised gun-vault requirements, Escudero asked whether a locked room could count. Sangalang declined to speculate about the rules or an inspected vault but agreed such storage was possible.

Sen. Joel Villanueva questioned PhilGEPS witness Rendell P. Sopeña about whether its records could show competition for GenCorp awards, including how often GenCorp was the only responsive bidder or repeatedly won similar contracts from the same procuring entity. Sopeña said he had no complete sole-bidder list; details could appear in uploaded documents, but the electronic data he downloaded did not expose those patterns.
