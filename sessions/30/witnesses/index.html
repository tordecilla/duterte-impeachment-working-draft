01

Rendell P. Sopeña

PhilGEPS E-Government Procurement Operations Division Chief

Sopeña confirmed that he remained under his prior oath during defense cross-examination; the chair later excused him.

00:38:53 · S0006800:39:06 · S0006900:39:08 · S0007001:38:44 · S00527

The PhilGEPS extract was described in questioning as 49 GenCorp award entries. Sopeña confirmed 15 awarded by Davao City and 33 by PhilHealth Region XI. He corrected counsel's description of the last entry as awarded by OWWA: it was awarded to OWWA by a procuring entity. Procuring entities upload the data; PhilGEPS is a repository, not the overseer of bidding or compliance.

Police Major Joericson H. Sangalang

PNP Firearms and Explosives Office Records Section Chief

Sangalang was sworn before recess; after limited stipulations and argument over relevance, the chair allowed examination on the remaining offered purposes. He was later excused.

01:43:20 · S0055801:43:51 · S0056002:43:39 · S0057502:59:32 · S0064302:59:38 · S00643B02:59:39 · S0064502:59:48 · S00645B02:59:49 · S00645C02:59:53 · S0064603:00:06 · S0064803:37:30 · S00961

Police Major Joericson H. Sangalang, PNP Firearms and Explosives Office records chief, testified that the office's records showed Type 5 licenses for both the Vice President and Atty. Carpio, with 22 firearms registered to her and 29 to him. He identified three of her rifles and one of his as Class A light weapons and described the certifications he brought as his source.
