Witnesses and evidence

Davao City Mayor Sebastian Zimmerman Duterte testified about GenCorp Industries Inc. procurement records brought under a court subpoena. He corrected an initial count of 15 awards to 19 city records. After the recess, counsel compared the records with earlier PhilGEPS testimony and identified four purchase orders outside the earlier presentation.

Court rulings and directions

The presiding officer declared Mayor Duterte a hostile witness, allowed the prosecution to use leading questions, and directed counsel not to mislead him.

00:31:14 · S0012900:31:26 · S0013100:31:28 · S00132

On the prosecution’s 104-item request for admission, the court sustained the respondent’s objection to applying Rule 26. The respondent need not answer; silence would not count as admission. The court denied expungement, left the papers in the record without evidentiary, factual or prejudicial effect against her, and did not decide the marital or filial privilege objection.

Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Raffy T. Tulfo said he would set aside his earlier conflict-of-interest question after the presiding officer discussed Sara Duterte’s reported GenCorp interest in her SALN and the absence of her name from an earlier SEC shareholder record, and Mayor Sebastian Duterte said he would not permit an award to GenCorp if he knew of that interest. Tulfo instead questioned GenCorp's contract eligibility. He read a 2024 procurement requirement that a supplier operate in the same business line for at least five years, contrasted it with GenCorp's 2020 registration and awards through 2025, and repeatedly asked when the BAC might have changed that rule. The mayor said the BAC may have modified the requirement but did not know when, would check with it, and had not discussed those specific procurement details with BAC officials.

Sen. Risa Hontiveros questioned Mayor Sebastian Zimmerman Duterte about his authorization, through a representative, of Davao City's GenCorp contracts worth more than ₱30 million. Sen. Panfilo M. Lacson asked Mayor Sebastian Duterte whether a lawyer had briefed him on the Arias v. Sandiganbayan doctrine before his testimony. The mayor said no. Lacson then observed that the mayor had not denied questions about contracts and his dealings with the BAC, and described the doctrine as allowing office heads to rely in good faith on subordinates' processing and recommendations. The mayor affirmed Lacson's characterization of his earlier answers.

Sen. Erwin Tulfo asked Mayor Sebastian Zimmerman Duterte what kinds of documents he signs and whether he relies on prior review and countersignatures. The mayor listed payroll, contracts and ordinances, and said he signs after the city administrator has checked and countersigned documents. Tulfo explicitly offered a suggestion and manifestation that the mayor question his staff on the GenCorp matter when he returned to Davao, warning that inadequate checking could expose him to trouble. Asked whether he saw a legal breach or would examine the matter himself, the mayor said he did not know, referred the decision to the court, and said he saw nothing if his sister's name was absent from the contract. Later, during Sen. Joel Villanueva's questions, Tulfo clarified the mayor's estimate of 50 to 300 documents: the mayor confirmed that the figure was per day, depending on bulk arrivals.
