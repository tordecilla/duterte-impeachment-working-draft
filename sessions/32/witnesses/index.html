Atty. Cathy Florence M. Baldonado

Register of Deeds IV, Davao City; prosecution witness

Atty. Cathy Florence M. Baldonado, Register of Deeds of Davao City, produced certified copies of Davao TCT No. 146-2024-010775, its sale deed, tax clearance and transfer-tax records, land and building tax declarations, electronic registration certificate and canceled predecessor title. She described the security features of certified copies. The court ordered copies furnished to both sides and later marking as Exhibit P-II-4-1 and series.

Baldonado described an active title for 379 square meters in Matina, Davao City, registered to Manases Carpio, married to Sara Duterte. Supporting records placed it in Huna Subdivision, Matina Crossing, Talomo. She read a P6 million sale consideration and clarified the deed date as July 2, 2024. She also read an August 27, 2024 mortgage annotation executed by Carpio and Duterte in favor of Philippine Savings Bank for P8 million.

On cross-examination Baldonado read newer tax declarations bearing the same title number: P1,864,680 land market value, P6,992,847.80 building market value and P82,046 land assessed value. 02

Atty. Marco K. Pineda

Deputy Register of Deeds III; acting Register of Deeds, Island Garden City of Samal; assisting Register of Deeds, Davao City; prosecution witness

Atty. Marco K. Pineda, Deputy Register of Deeds III designated Acting Register of Deeds of Island Garden City of Samal and concurrently Assisting Register of Deeds of Davao City, explained the registry's ministerial custody and registration functions. The defense stipulated to certified copies and to the Samal title's 751-square-meter area, Matanos location and wording naming Carpio, married to Duterte. It stipulated to the annotated P3,453,849 price; Pineda read March 15, 2018 as the deed date and said TCT No. 142-2019-013134 remained active. The court ordered later marking as Exhibit P-II-4-2 and series.

Pineda said the SALN entries shown on direct did not perfectly match the deed and limited his answers to reading the documents. On cross he read San Isidro, Kaputian District, in the deed and Matanos in the title issued March 28, 2019. He read a San Isidro spouse entry in the 2018 SALN and a Matanos entry in 2019, each recording 2013 acquisition and P4,041,000 cost. He declined to identify the property from a quick visual comparison.
