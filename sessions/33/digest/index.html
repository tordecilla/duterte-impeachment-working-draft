Witnesses and evidence

AMLC Executive Director Atty. Ronel U. Buenaventura, head of the Anti-Money Laundering Council Secretariat, testified about reports from banks and other covered persons. He identified subpoenaed records and a prosecution-requested tabular summary, prepared by analysts and reviewing officers, which he reviewed and signed. Atty. Mae Sancho Divinagracia conducted direct examination; Atty. Mark C. Vinluan began cross-examination after she concluded.

Court rulings and directions

Presiding Officer Sen. Francis "Chiz" G. Escudero denied the defense motion to exclude Buenaventura. Citing Republic v. Sandiganbayan, he reasoned that its interpretation of the unchanged Section 9C of AMLA remained authoritative despite Republic Act No. 11521's addition of Section 8A. He read the newer confidentiality duty together with AMLC's statutory functions and information-exchange authority, rather than as absolute immunity from lawful compulsory process. He also cited PNB v. Gancayco on the bank-secrecy law's impeachment exception as support for constitutional accountability, while distinguishing that exception from the separate confidentiality of AMLC information. The witness could identify, authenticate and explain subpoenaed reports on relevant matters, with in-camera testimony when necessary and further evidentiary objections available; the ruling did not authorize unrestricted public disclosure.

The chair permitted earlier transactions as a net-worth baseline while urging counsel to reach the vice-presidential period. He clarified that the limitation on pre-June 30, 2022 material concerned establishing another impeachable offense.

03:47:55 · S0056004:15:26 · S0066103:59:42 · S0060703:59:56 · S00607A03:59:58 · S00608

The chair overruled Sen. Imee Marcos's foreign-currency objection, distinguishing testimony about reports lawfully held by AMLC from compelling banks to disclose foreign-currency deposits. He read Republic v. He also cited Salvacion v. Central Bank and China Banking Corporation v. Court of Appeals as examples of exceptions to foreign-currency secrecy where rigid application would perpetuate injustice. He directed the witness to continue under that ruling.
