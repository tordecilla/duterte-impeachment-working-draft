Earlier House submission

Signatures and signoffs redacted for data privacy.

The archived House packet contains the AMLC transmittal and aggregate figures, covered- and suspicious-transaction extracts, a financial-intelligence report, and information-sharing material with a historical search. During the October 5 exchange, Buenaventura distinguished the earlier 2006–2025 banks-only coverage from the court subpoena's 2007–2025 coverage of all covered persons, and explained that corrections to 13 covered-transaction reports had been incorporated into the July 30 court submission. This earlier packet is presented separately from the July 30 and October 1 court submissions.
