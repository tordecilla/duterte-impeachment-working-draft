Court rulings and directions

Presiding Officer Sen. Francis "Chiz" G. Escudero initially required ordinary document identification for Sumatra and recognized the defense's foundation objections. After consulting both sides during recess, he distinguished Republic v. Marcos, where collection and custody alone did not authenticate private documents, and adopted an abbreviated procedure for Dela Cerna: direct bank production under subpoena, documented custody, inspection and court-supervised reproduction established provenance, identity, integrity and fidelity of copies. Specific substantial challenges could still support exclusion, limits on disputed entries or testimony from an appropriate custodian during cross-examination or formal offer.

Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Francis "Chiz" G. Escudero asked how banks decide whether a transaction is suspicious. Buenaventura said the assessment is subjective but constrained by legal criteria, client profiles, transaction patterns and regulatory red flags, with documented processes subject to audit. Asked whether the STRs read the previous day cited newspaper reports, he could not recall without seeing the documents. Escudero initially suggested Tapang at Malasakit lacked juridical personality and asked whether it appeared in the defense's displayed article; Vinluan said it did not. Escudero later expressly corrected himself: Tapang at Malasakit Alliance for the Philippines Incorporated was an SEC-registered foundation whose accounts or investments could potentially fall within AMLC coverage. In his final questioning, Escudero separated the approximately ₱529.8 million in reported time-deposit transactions from the ₱4.4 billion aggregate. Buenaventura confirmed that the time-deposit amount was included in the larger total and clarified that its 2007–2025 total was not wholly included in the shorter 2022–2025 period. He agreed this was the subset relevant to the parties' rollover discussion, while expressly declining to say the transactions actually were rollovers. Escudero also asked whether Gencorp's larger reported outflows than inflows could indicate incoming transactions absent from the CTR/STR records. Buenaventura accepted that only as a possibility from his initial personal reading, rather than an official AMLC analysis. Escudero then tested hypothetical comparisons between reported transaction totals and sums of successive SALNs: approximately ₱424 million in transactions for 2022–2025 against his ₱336 million sum, and ₱4.4 billion for 2007–2025 against his ₱840 million sum. He acknowledged that adding annual SALN declarations was not the proper comparison, and also posed a salary-only scenario using ₱350,000 a month. Buenaventura explained that SALNs concern year-end assets while the reports describe transactions across the years; he had not seen the SALNs and could not explain the totals on Escudero's premises.
