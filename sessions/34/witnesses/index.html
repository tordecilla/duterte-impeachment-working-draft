AMLC Executive Director Atty. Ronel U. Buenaventura

Executive Director, Anti-Money Laundering Council Secretariat; prosecution witness

Buenaventura continued testimony under the oath administered the previous day. Defense cross-examination, prosecution redirect, defense recross and senator-judge questions were completed, and the court excused him.

Buenaventura confirmed billions in reported inflows and outflows, including the PHP4.4-billion aggregate, while explaining that transaction totals did not give cash balances. AMLC had not witnessed the underlying transactions; further analysis and investigation were needed to determine legality.

00:43:38 · S0024900:44:28 · S00254C00:44:59 · S00257C01:14:27 · S0042001:04:27 · S00377

Buenaventura read a BPI letter requesting deletion of 13 covered-transaction reports affected by a system bug. The confidential information-sharing report said that, assuming the requested correction was accurate, manager's checks procured by NAI for Carpio would total PHP 2,110,714.29 rather than PHP 2,334,617,151.92. He confirmed that the erroneous PHP 2-billion amount was excluded from the tabulation. He also explained that duplicate reports for jointly held accounts were deducted, with PHP 81 million identified in Annex A.

For Cale88 Foods Corporation, Buenaventura confirmed PHP 1,254,736,297.08 in aggregate reported transactions and PHP 776,643,950.36 in inflows. He read three STR entries citing no underlying legal or trade obligation, purpose or economic justification. He said the inward-remittance entries were unique transactions rather than time-deposit rollovers, and that their country classifications used the remitters' reported addresses rather than correspondent-bank locations.

He described the submitted financial-intelligence, financial-investigation and information-sharing reports, but declined to disclose other actions beyond the subpoenaed material, citing Court of Appeals bank-inquiry confidentiality rules.

Asked to identify a specific unexplained amount, he left that determination to the impeachment court. In response to Escudero's hypothetical comparisons with SALN and salary figures, he distinguished year-end assets from transactions across years; Escudero acknowledged that adding successive annual SALNs was inappropriate.

Eunice C. Sumatra

Branch Head, LANDBANK San Pedro Davao Branch; prosecution witness

Sumatra took the witness oath and identified herself as branch head of LANDBANK San Pedro Davao. She testified on the bank records produced pursuant to subpoena.

04:56:35 · S0145604:56:56 · S0145805:07:55 · S0149905:09:00 · S0150405:09:36 · S01507

Eunice C. Sumatra, branch head of Land Bank's San Pedro Davao branch, identified Duterte's single payroll account at that branch, its movement records for 2017 to 2025 and her signatures on the 21-page record. She also identified withdrawal and on-us check-deposit documents, explaining that transactions at other branches had certifications from those branches. On cross-examination, she confirmed government payroll was credited to the account.

Raquel U. Dela Cerna

Branch Manager, LANDBANK Davao R. Magsaysay Branch; prosecution witness

Dela Cerna took the witness oath, identified herself as branch manager of LANDBANK Davao R. Magsaysay, and explained her supervision of branch operations and implementation of internal controls.

06:53:31 · S0194806:53:51 · S01949B06:58:55 · S0196506:59:19 · S01969

Raquel U. Dela Cerna, branch manager of Land Bank's Davao R. Magsaysay branch, said Duterte's two accounts there were closed and Carpio's account ending 2730 remained active. She gave its year-end balances as PHP 505,517.35 in 2022, PHP 953,681.42 in 2023, PHP 115,824.20 in 2024 and PHP 1,358,373.07 in 2025.

06:58:55 · S0196507:00:12 · S0197107:00:33 · S0197307:01:03 · S0197907:01:37 · S0198607:01:59 · S0198807:02:18 · S0199007:02:35 · S01992

On defense cross-examination, Dela Cerna said the bank did not automatically send account notices or balances to a spouse. It required a special power of attorney for release of account information, and marriage alone did not confer signatory authority, online access or entitlement to the other spouse's balance.
