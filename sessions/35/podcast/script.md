## How the BPI checks worked

Galvez explained that time deposits count toward total balances, and matured proceeds go into a settlement account. Buying a funded manager's check debits the purchaser's account and moves the backing money to the bank's settlement account. The amount therefore leaves the customer's visible deposit balance before the check is encashed.

Expiration alone does not return it. The original unused check must be returned; reissuing a check debits the funds again. Senator Chiz Escudero explored those mechanics. Senator Panfilo Lacson asked whether the prosecution was arguing concealment through circulating checks, and counsel James Bryan Ibrahim A. Alih agreed. But Galvez could not confirm deliberate year-end concealment or explain the clients' purposes. Senator Bam Aquino's questions about deliberate six-month cycling produced no definitive answer about legality.
