Atty. Anne Loraine Garcia-Marquez

Chief of Staff, Office of the BIR Commissioner; prosecution witness

Garcia-Marquez testified under oath about tax records submitted for Vice President Sara Duterte, her husband Atty. Manases R. Carpio, and identified business entities. A certified public accountant and lawyer, she said her office reviews documents for the BIR commissioner and submitted the records required by the court’s subpoena.

05:11:09 · S0014505:11:25 · S0014605:21:41 · S0018805:22:54 · S0019505:23:33 · S0019905:24:28 · S0020305:24:44 · S00205

Garcia-Marquez said the October 6 summaries answered the prosecution panel’s request for information in filed annual income tax returns. Revenue district staff encoded figures; technical assistants in the commissioner’s office, including her, reviewed them through two levels of review, and she finalized the summaries.

05:30:24 · S0023405:30:38 · S0023505:31:07 · S0023705:55:09 · S0031805:55:42 · S0032005:55:51 · S0032205:55:57 · S00324

Reading Annex A, Garcia-Marquez put the spouses’ combined income net of expenses and tax for 2007–2025 at ₱85,343,803.23. She said the non-taxable-income and income-subject-to-final-tax lines contained no reported amounts in the filed returns.

05:57:20 · S0034605:57:49 · S0034805:58:35 · S0035305:58:45 · S0035405:58:50 · S0035505:59:26 · S0035905:59:58 · S00362B06:01:04 · S00371

From the spouses’ joint audited financial statements, Garcia-Marquez read cash on hand and in banks of ₱21,234,980 at December 31, 2022; ₱28,971,725 at December 31, 2023; ₱15,525,209 at December 31, 2024; and ₱13,804,540 at December 31, 2025. She explained that cash has its own line item in financial statements.

During Atty. Erwin G. Matib’s questions about Duterte’s other income, Garcia-Marquez read ₱1,082,800 for 2025 and ₱1,058,021 for 2024 from the joint financial statements, apart from compensation income. She said the statements did not identify that income’s source; she associated it with legal practice because Duterte’s BIR registration listed her as a professional engaged in legal activities. Atty. Kristine R. Ferrer challenged that inference, and the chair noted that Garcia-Marquez had not been qualified as an expert.

Garcia-Marquez said both lawful and unlawful income are taxable. Answering the chair, she confirmed that a letter of authority had been issued in relation to Duterte or her spouse, but said its details were confidential and outside the subpoenaed documents.

For 2021, Garcia-Marquez read gross compensation income of ₱2,361,252 and sales, revenues, receipts or fees of ₱4,650,000. She also read ₱3,188,776 in net sales, revenues, receipts or fees for 2017, ₱1,876,417 for 2018, and ₱4,051,000 for 2019, in addition to compensation income. She confirmed that the returns used those revenue labels and that her association with legal practice came from the registration documents. The court limited pre-vice-presidency records to baseline or pattern evidence.

06:28:57 · S0059406:30:06 · S0059906:30:27 · S0060106:30:35 · S0060206:32:43 · S0061306:33:06 · S0061506:33:44 · S0061706:33:51 · S0061806:40:44 · S0066806:41:05 · S0067006:41:26 · S0067206:41:47 · S00673B

Garcia-Marquez read a BIR certification reporting no database record of a transfer, sale or disposition of Manases Carpio’s Cale88 Foods Corporation shares, or related tax payments, assessments or settlements. She said the BIR had issued no eCAR and had no pending application. She explained that BIR rules require the tax-payment record before registering a share transfer in the corporation’s books. Asked about validity, she said a transfer may be valid between the parties and referred the contractual question to the law on contracts.
