Witnesses and evidence

Atty. Alexander B. Cabrera, a lawyer and CPA appearing as a prosecution witness, said prosecutors engaged him for an Article II financial review. He worked pro bono in his personal capacity, independently and without promising a predetermined result. His report compared the spouses’ SALNs, bank accounts, tax records, AMLC reports, corporate filings and financial statements supplied by prosecutors.

Cabrera’s Table 39 calculated P190,628,012 for 2022, P167,337,227 for 2023, P264,217,123 for 2024 and P195,692,505 for 2025, totaling P817,874,867 in undeclared assets and income.
