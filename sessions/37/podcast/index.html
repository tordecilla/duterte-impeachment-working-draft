Exclusions and corporate estimates

Cabrera excluded loan proceeds, rolling time deposits, investment proceeds and transfers between savings and checking from his income calculation. His information did not cover cash outside banks or digital wallets; he said dollar accounts were excluded apart from some Cale88 information. For corporate inflows, he applied ownership percentages through his analysis of piercing the corporate veil. He reported average annual understatements of sixty-nine percent for Metro City Chow, fifty-one percent for Cale88 and eighty-seven percent for GenCorp. Metro City Chow's corporate filing gave Duterte a twenty-percent interest. For GenCorp, her SALN listed a stockholding but the corporate filing did not name her. Cabrera used a twenty-percent sensitivity estimate, citing accounting and SEC benchmarks and the related ownership and directorships.
