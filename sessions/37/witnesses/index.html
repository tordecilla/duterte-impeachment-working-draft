01

Atty. Alexander B. Cabrera

Lawyer and CPA; prosecution financial expert witness

Atty. Alexander B. Cabrera, a lawyer and CPA appearing as a prosecution witness, testified under oath that prosecutors engaged him to examine financial, corporate and tax records concerning Article II. He said he worked pro bono in his personal capacity, independently and without promising a predetermined result. His Forensic Report on Vice President Sara Duterte and Spouse's Financial, Corporate, Property, and Related Records compared SALNs, bank accounts, tax returns, AMLC reports, corporate filings and financial statements supplied by prosecutors. He combined the spouses' records because the SALN covered the spouse's property and, in his analysis, their property regime was absolute community.

Cabrera's Table 39 calculated P190,628,012 for 2022, P167,337,227 for 2023, P264,217,123 for 2024 and P195,692,505 for 2025, totaling P817,874,867 in undeclared assets and income. 02

Atty. Anne Loraine Garcia-Marquez

Chief of Staff, Office of the BIR Commissioner; prosecution witness

Atty. Anne Loraine Garcia-Marquez, Chief of Staff in the Office of the BIR Commissioner and a prosecution witness, testified that the income-tax audit of Vice President Sara Zimmerman Duterte and her husband was ongoing. She said Cale88 registered with the BIR on October 15, 2021, and reported P208,493,545 in sales for 2021–2025. Bank records were outside the ordinary letter-of-authority checklist because of bank secrecy, although taxpayers could submit them voluntarily and the BIR coordinated with the AMLC. She confirmed that Cale88 already had a letter of authority and that its supporting invoices and expense records would be checked in the audit.

Garcia-Marquez read P82,327.45 in gross compensation on Duterte's BIR Form 2316 from the City of Davao, and clarified that the document concerned taxable year 2023 and was filed on April 11, 2024.
