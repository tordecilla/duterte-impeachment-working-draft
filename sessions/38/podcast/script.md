## The documents and method

Cabrera described examining statements of assets, liabilities and net worth, known as SALNs, alongside property records, insurance and investment information, income-tax returns, corporate statements and Anti-Money Laundering Council material. His corporate review focused on GenCorp, Metro City Chow and Cale88. He compared bank balances against declared cash, using zero where the SALN had no cash entry. He accepted that cash could have been classified elsewhere, but said that reclassification would not remove the difference in his total-property comparison. He combined the spouses' property under SALN requirements and presumed absolute community of property, saying his team had received no contrary marital-property documents. To avoid duplicates, the team compared accounts, used multiple reviewers and traced fresh inflows behind annual ending balances.
