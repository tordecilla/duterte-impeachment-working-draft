Atty. Alexander B. Cabrera

Lawyer and CPA; prosecution witness

Atty. Alexander B. Cabrera, a lawyer and CPA appearing as the prosecution's forensic-accounting witness, continued under his earlier oath and defended his October 2 report estimating P817,874,867 in unexplained wealth for 2022-2025.
