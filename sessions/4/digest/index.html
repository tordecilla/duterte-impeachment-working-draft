At a glance

  • Bottom line: COA auditor Roderick Wamil spent August 3 testifying about his audit of the OVP's fourth-quarter 2022 confidential-fund records, but his direct examination did not finish.
  • What the witness said: Wamil testified that OVP plans and reports lacked required detail or support and that his acknowledgment-receipt summary showed P125.15 million in expenses, including receipts with dates outside the covered period.
  • Defense challenge: The defense declined to stipulate to all 845 acknowledgment receipts and reserved challenges to their provenance, the unsigned summary's accuracy, and the scope of Wamil's opinions.
  • Court's limits: The court allowed a voluminous-record summary while separately limiting Wamil to his audit evaluation, not expert conclusions about confidentiality, regularity, or violations of Republic Act 3019.
  • What's next: Wamil remained under the same oath for continued direct examination on August 4, with the remaining examination of the OVP's first three 2023 quarters, the DepEd material, and defense cross-examination still ahead.

Witnesses and evidence

Wamil identified himself as a COA audit team leader, lawyer, and accountant with more than 11 years at the commission and prior service as a State Auditor in the Intelligence and Confidential Fund Audit Office.

01:21:44 · S0019201:22:02 · S0019501:22:28 · S0020101:23:04 · S00207

Wamil described his work as a compliance post-audit conducted after disbursement under Joint Circular 2015-01. He said he audited OVP confidential funds totaling P500 million across four quarters and DepEd funds totaling P112.5 million across three quarters.

01:25:09 · S0022101:26:12 · S0022301:39:10 · S0030901:39:37 · S00311

Wamil testified that the OVP physical and financial plan and initial accomplishment report did not identify specific confidential activities. He also said the disbursement voucher referred to calendar year 2022 even though the plan concerned the fourth quarter, while the liquidation report covered only December 21-31.
