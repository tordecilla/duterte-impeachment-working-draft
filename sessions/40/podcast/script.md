## First defense witness

The central question was whether a disputed Matina Crossing property appeared in the Vice President's 2024 and 2025 statements of assets, liabilities and net worth, or SALNs. Adalin produced five tax declarations, three maps and a barangay certification. He initially identified the properties as the same, then qualified that identification under questioning about the records and other possible holdings.
