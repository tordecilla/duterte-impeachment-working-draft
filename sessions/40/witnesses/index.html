Engr. Jaime G. Adalin

Davao City Assessor; defense witness

Adalin, a civil engineer and real-estate appraiser, testified as the defense’s first witness and produced five tax declarations, three maps and a barangay certification concerning the property covered by title ending 0775.

He explained how the maps linked the assessment records to the physical property: the district map placed it in Talomo, the barangay map identified Matina Crossing as Barangay 74-A, and the property identification map connected the tax declaration’s property index number and parcel count 910 to title ending 0775.

Adalin read Manases Carpio, married to Sara Duterte, as the registered owner. The current land declaration showed PHP 82,046 assessed value and PHP 1,864,680 market value; the building on the same titled lot showed PHP 5,244,635.85 assessed value and PHP 6,992,847.80 market value.

00:54:45 · S0025800:55:28 · S00264_E100:55:37 · S0026800:55:46 · S0027000:57:54 · S00285_E100:58:03 · S0028700:58:24 · S0029100:58:32 · S0029300:58:43 · S00295

He initially identified the Barangay 74-A entries in the 2024 and 2025 SALNs with the titled land and building, relying on location and matching assessment figures. Later, he agreed that he was not certain, had checked only the subpoenaed title, and could verify the records; he subsequently described the SALN match as most likely.

On cross-examination, Adalin read PHP 10 million as the 2024 SALN acquisition cost and agreed that the eCAR stated a PHP 6 million selling price, named Manases Carpio as buyer and included an improvement. The SALN entry before him described a residential lot. He acknowledged that the SALN lacked exact coordinates and block or lot numbers.

Adalin distinguished ordinance-based assessment and market values from the buyer-seller transaction price. He said the assessor did not verify the amount actually paid and that determining purchase price was outside his office’s jurisdiction. On redirect, he identified the SALN’s assessed-value, fair-market-value and acquisition-cost fields and agreed that it had no purchase-price field.

Adalin initially could not identify the street-view and Prime Bistro photographs as the property. Later, he said technical staff had confirmed it and identified an image without the business sign as matching his appraisers’ photograph, explaining that the unfamiliar sign had caused his uncertainty.

After checking with his office, Adalin acknowledged China travel in January 2026 and described it as personal and self-funded. He recalled the city administrator, engineer and treasurer among his companions. Asked about a 2018 trip, he recalled sister-city travel and offered official documents; the chair directed the clerk to coordinate on travel information.
