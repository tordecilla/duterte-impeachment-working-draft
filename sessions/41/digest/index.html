At a glance

  • Principal testimony: The defense called Engr. John Kenneth D. Enumerables, Island Garden City of Samal City Assessor, on property disclosure, and Vingeline A. Bahan, Davao City Accountant, on a payment for earlier mayoral service.
  • Samal dispute: The defense relied on matching assessor values and the title chain; prosecutors challenged the location descriptions, 2013 acquisition year and P4,041,000 SALN cost declared by Vice President Sara Zimmerman Duterte against the 2018 deed and P3,453,849 price.

The defense offered his testimony to connect TCT 142-2019-013134 to the Vice President's SALN entry. Prosecutor Benjamin Tolosa Jr. stipulated to his position and challenged the proposed property identification.
