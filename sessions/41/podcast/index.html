02

Two defense witnesses

The defense presentation centered on two witnesses: Engr. John Kenneth D. Enumerables, Island Garden City of Samal City Assessor, and Vingeline A. Bahan, Davao City Accountant. Enumerables addressed whether a titled Samal property matched an entry in the Vice President's statement of assets, liabilities and net worth, or SALN. Bahan explained a payment made in 2023 as salary differentials for Sara Duterte's earlier service as Davao mayor. Prosecutors challenged the property match and the payment records. Extensive senator-judge questioning also examined Bahan's former private accounting partnership and her independence.

What the Samal records showed

Enumerables identified Manases Reyes Carpio as the declared owner of the parcel under title number 142-2019-013134. His office placed it in Barangay San Isidro, Kaputian, in the Island Garden City of Samal. He presented tax declarations, appraisal records, maps and the title. The earlier declaration carried assessed value of 270,360 pesos and market value of 2,703,600 pesos. The replacement after the 2023 general revision carried assessed value of 346,960 pesos and market value of 4,956,600 pesos. The defense relied on those values to connect the parcel to the SALN entry.

06

Different dates and amounts

The prosecution emphasized another set of differences. The SALN stated acquisition in 2013 at 4,041,000 pesos. The deed was dated March 15, 2018 and gave a price of 3,453,849 pesos. Enumerables agreed that the titles had no annotated contract to sell. Prosecutor Atty. Benjamin Tolosa Jr. argued that the date and price differences supported separate properties, and invoked Civil Service Commission guidelines to argue that acquisition cost excluded taxes and transfer fees. Defense counsel Atty. Lindon Miguel C. Bacquel maintained that acquisition cost included transaction expenses and that the property had been disclosed. Enumerables said transfer expenses could possibly explain the higher amount. Asked about an alternative explanation involving the actual price paid, Bacquel declined to speculate.

The Davao salary explanation

Bahan's testimony moved the hearing to the double-compensation allegation. She identified net salary differentials of 31,718 pesos and 64 centavos for 2020 and 32,476 pesos and eight centavos for 2021, totaling 64,194 pesos and 72 centavos. Tax reporting and custody of originals

Prosecutor Atty. Mae Sancho Divinagracia tested the paper trail. The former accounting partnership

Much of Bahan's examination concerned her earlier accounting partnership. Prosecutors presented original and amended partnership articles. After difficulty recalling the old records, Bahan identified her signature and tax number and acknowledged that she had been a partner and later withdrawn. The records placed her partnership between 2003 and 2015, overlapping permanent city employment from 2004. Sen. Joel Villanueva read her affidavit recording resignation effective August 1, 2015. Bahan described herself as a partner in name rather than a functioning partner, said she received meeting allowances, and denied introducing the firm to the Dutertes. On redirect, the defense elicited that the capital listed under her name was 1,000 pesos out of 19,000 pesos and described that contribution as nominal.
