Engr. John Kenneth D. Enumerables

Island Garden City of Samal City Assessor; defense witness

Engr. John Kenneth D. Enumerables, Island Garden City of Samal City Assessor, testified as a licensed civil engineer and real estate appraiser. The defense offered his testimony to connect TCT 142-2019-013134 to the Vice President's SALN entry. Prosecutor Benjamin Tolosa Jr. stipulated to his position and challenged the proposed property identification.

00:17:50 · S0005400:19:13 · S0006000:22:22 · S0007900:22:33 · S0008300:23:05 · S00089

Enumerables identified Manases Reyes Carpio as the declared owner of the parcel under TCT 142-2019-013134, located in his office's records at Barangay San Isidro, Kaputian, Island Garden City of Samal. The 2017 tax declaration carried an assessed value of P270,360 and market value of P2,703,600; it was cancelled in the 2023 general revision. The replacement carried P346,960 assessed value and P4,956,600 market value, the latter clarified after an initial misstatement. The defense marked the declarations and associated appraisal sheets, title, maps and control rolls.

00:53:26 · S0032500:54:00 · S0032900:54:13 · S0033100:54:21 · S0033300:54:34 · S0033500:54:43 · S0033700:56:16 · S0035200:56:31 · S0035600:56:43 · S0035700:52:05 · S0031800:48:15 · S0028400:46:07 · S00272

Enumerables initially linked the SALN entry to the title on the basis of matching assessed and market values, saying he had no other basis because the SALN lacked title and property-record numbers. He said his official location was San Isidro, there was no Barangay Matanos in Samal, and he was unfamiliar with Matanos. On cross he said he was not certain the SALN and title described the same property. Later, answering a senator-judge question based on his professional knowledge and office records, he said there was only one parcel.

01:03:24 · S0039901:03:48 · S0040301:04:31 · S0040901:04:51 · S0041301:04:58 · S0041501:05:16 · S00420_E201:17:17 · S00507_E301:17:27 · S00507_E401:43:08 · S0072801:43:43 · S00729

On redirect, Enumerables read the deed's description of Lot 11, Block 1, Phase 1, Kembali Coast Subdivision Village in San Isidro, tied its previous title T-244681 to the title cancelled by TCT 142-2019-013134, and read Matanos from the current title. He said Kembali Coast titles used Matanos while the assessor's mapping placed the development in San Isidro. The chair consulted the October 1 testimony of Acting Register of Deeds Marco Pineda and reported that the Matanos description was carried forward from the mother title, with tax mapping needed to identify the physical parcel.

01:19:47 · S0054401:20:10 · S0054601:20:34 · S0054801:21:18 · S0055501:36:43 · S0067301:36:56 · S0067701:43:50 · S00731

Enumerables agreed that the SALN listed P4,041,000 and acquisition year 2013, while the deed showed P3,453,849 and March 15, 2018; the titles bore no contract-to-sell annotation. He explained acquisition cost as the purchase price plus transfer expenses and said such costs could possibly explain the higher amount. Tolosa argued that CSC guidelines excluded taxes and transfer fees and that the different dates and amounts supported the prosecution's identification of separate properties. Defense counsel maintained that acquisition cost included transaction expenses and that the parcel was the same one declared in the SALN.

Prosecutors adopted the previous day's descriptions of the types of records; the chair allowed local explanations while dispensing with authentication and execution. When Tolosa separately challenged the unsigned, undated photocopy of the second control-roll document, Enumerables said it was generated by his office's computer, and the chair ruled on its authenticity on that basis.

02

Vingeline A. Bahan

Davao City Accountant; defense witness

Vingeline A. Bahan was called to the witness stand and took the oath before her direct examination.

03:03:57 · S0088103:04:37 · S0088503:04:43 · S0088603:04:57 · S00887

Vingeline A. Bahan, Davao City Accountant, identified P64,194.72 as salary differentials for Duterte's service as city mayor in 2020 and 2021 under the salary-standardization increases. The defense offered the account against the double-compensation allegation. Prosecutor Mae Sancho Divinagracia stipulated to Bahan's position and functions and reserved the remaining testimony for cross-examination.

03:06:54 · S0090503:07:39 · S0090803:08:54 · S00919_M0303:11:51 · S0095303:13:26 · S0096803:36:00 · S0115503:36:05 · S01156_M0203:36:15 · S01159

Bahan identified a January 9, 2023 summary list of payroll, the 2020 and 2021 salary-differential registers, a December 30, 2022 disbursement voucher, a Land Bank receipt dated January 11, 2023 and cheque 0002553018, and notices of salary adjustment. She gave the registers' net amounts as P31,718.64 and P32,476.08, totaling P64,194.72. She said the receipt showed Land Bank's receipt of the cheque and the payment was for credit through payroll. The defense requested exhibit markings, which the chair ordered after clarifying handwriting.

03:15:14 · S0098103:17:53 · S0100303:18:41 · S0100803:19:21 · S0101403:19:59 · S0102003:20:23 · S0102203:20:29 · S0102303:21:55 · S0103603:23:14 · S0105503:24:28 · S0105803:26:22 · S01073

Bahan attributed the handwritten corrections and countersignatures to Assistant City Accountant Janice Antoinette G. Calza. She said the typed 2022 period was wrong and was corrected to January-December 2020 and January-December 2021 during accounting evaluation on December 30, 2022, to conform to the supporting records. A senator-judge challenged the timing and suggested the entries could have been changed later; Bahan maintained that the correction occurred during voucher preparation and evaluation.

03:25:36 · S0106103:28:24 · S0107803:28:43 · S0107903:29:04 · S0108303:29:19 · S0109003:29:23 · S0109103:29:54 · S0110403:30:00 · S0110605:24:03 · S01911

Bahan said the cheque was issued January 10, 2023 after supporting documents were completed. She described the adjustments as apparently overlooked, placed preparation with the City Mayor's Office and evaluation with accounting, and could not identify which particular document had been missing because staff handled evaluation. She distinguished compensation paid in 2023 from compensation for 2023 and said the payment was for the 2020-2021 mayoral service, with no other Davao compensation paid from 2023 to 2025.

03:32:26 · S0112003:32:41 · S0112204:35:35 · S0151204:36:02 · S0152004:38:53 · S0154205:03:20 · S01744_M0205:21:30 · S0189305:22:20 · S0189905:23:39 · S01908

Bahan acknowledged signing the 2023 Form 2316 and agreed that the form itself did not specify a prior service period, payroll, voucher or salary-differential basis. Prosecutors compared P881,012 in the 2022 Form 2316 with P938,512 in the city's 2022 financial statements and elicited that their difference did not match the 2023 payment. Bahan explained that the financial-statement figure came from HRMO and represented gross pay, whereas the BIR report reflected deductions including leave without pay.

On cross she had no transmittal proof with her and the copies bore no COA receipt stamp. On redirect she said the salary differential had drawn no audit-observation memorandum, suspension or disallowance.

Prosecutors confronted Bahan with the 2003 articles of Ampil, Elises, Ciudadano & Co. CPAs and the 2015 amended articles of Elises and Ciudadano. Bahan eventually identified her signature and tax number, agreed she had been a partner and had withdrawn, and read the SEC certification recording the amended articles on September 1, 2015. Joel Villanueva read her withdrawal affidavit stating resignation effective August 1, 2015 for personal reasons.

Bahan described herself as a partner in name rather than a functioning partner and said she received allowances for meetings. She traced her connection to college training with Ciudadano, said she had no knowledge of the firm's transactions and denied introducing the firm to the Dutertes. On redirect she identified P1,000 of the original P19,000 capital as listed under her name, while the three name partners held P5,000 each; she described the old documents as requiring time to refresh her memory. She said her active participation ceased when she joined the city accounting office, although she continued visiting.
