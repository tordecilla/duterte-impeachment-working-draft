Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Francis “Kiko” Pangilinan sought corporate privilege waivers to avoid a repeat of counsel-witness refusals to answer. The Chair presumed authorization and corporate waiver, barred selective invocation, and preserved privilege concerning individual shareholders; Punay accepted. Later, Pangilinan challenged her categorical denial of Duterte's GenCorp participation: Punay distinguished management from receiving investment disbursements and acknowledged Duterte as a recipient. Pangilinan questioned the reliability of her categorical assurances. In another round, he questioned GenCorp shareholding declared in the 2024-2025 SALNs despite Duterte not being a shareholder of record. Punay agreed the filing could be inaccurate or false, qualifying perjury as requiring intentional, deliberate conduct. She accepted investment receipts as a possible explanation; Pangilinan also identified a 2022-2023 disclosure gap given receipts throughout 2022-2025. He pressed for supporting payment records: Punay learned of the arrangement around July, requested extraction the previous week, and had only an accounting list. He supported obtaining the underlying documents. He also challenged her failure as general counsel to investigate management restrictions behind the auditor's qualified opinion on cash verification and inventory counting; she knew neither the restrictions nor had asked.
