Atty. Suzette Balucanag-Punay

Defense witness; in-house counsel of GenCorp Industries Inc. and Metro City Chow Foods Corporation

The defense called Balucanag-Punay to the witness stand; she took the oath and later was excused by the Chair.

00:10:10 · S0002500:10:42 · S0002700:10:58 · S0002808:00:00 · S42L_gap13_1

Atty. Suzette Balucanag-Punay, in-house counsel of GenCorp Industries Inc. and Metro City Chow Foods Corporation since mid-2022, appeared under Senate subpoena. She said her work covered legal inquiries, litigation assistance and employee discipline, with access to corporate records.

00:10:10 · S0002500:31:30 · S0009300:32:06 · S0009700:32:19 · S0009900:32:42 · S0010103:24:00 · S0092203:24:11 · S0092403:25:43 · S00933

Balucanag-Punay testified that GenCorp's 2022-2025 general information sheets accurately listed its stockholders and that the Vice President held no shares or corporate position. She disputed Atty. Alexander Cabrera's attribution of 20% ownership and P250,000 paid-up capital, citing GenCorp's records and total paid-up capital of P1.25 million.

00:43:47 · S0016000:44:06 · S0016300:51:39 · S0020300:51:51 · S0020500:52:04 · S0020701:02:00 · S0024701:02:14 · S0024901:02:32 · S0025101:03:07 · S0025601:03:23 · S0025801:03:38 · S0026001:15:11 · S0030601:15:54 · S00308_E0201:16:28 · S0031101:18:22 · S00322

She identified five Jollibee stores under GenCorp: Bolton, Matina Crossing, G-Mall, Times Square and Abriza, and described their gradual consolidation from predecessor corporations. She said the purpose was to centralize the stores under one corporation; the process began in 2021. She described the Vice President's "loose investment" as originating in Davao Bounty in 2007, when that company hoped to obtain the Times Square franchise. After that failed, she said the investment was carried through Great Jolly Times Foods Corporation and Times Square Bee Foods Corporation to GenCorp, remaining specific to the Times Square store.

Citing an accountant's transaction summary, she listed disbursements of P337,500 in 2022, P555,750 in 2023, P612,419.14 in 2024 and P636,700 in 2025, totaling P2,142,369.14. She called them return of capital and said GenCorp issued checks from the Times Square branch's bank account. She challenged Table 39's GenCorp undeclared-income figures of P55,714,292 for 2023, P74,056,480 for 2024 and P82,858,946 for 2025 by comparing them with the smaller disbursements in the accountant's summary.

02:03:22 · S0070302:08:14 · S0074802:08:29 · S0075002:09:02 · S0075402:09:53 · S0075902:10:19 · S0076102:10:44 · S0076302:13:34 · S0077802:13:38 · S0078002:13:42 · S0078102:02:15 · S0069702:02:56 · S0069802:03:07 · S0070102:08:35 · S0075102:08:59 · S0075202:09:16 · S0075502:09:38 · S0075602:09:48 · S00757

On questioning, she acknowledged having no written investment agreement or verifiable record of the full investment amount, no cancelled checks, and no personal knowledge of the payment formula or journal entries. Presented with Davao Bounty's articles showing P46,875 paid up, she had no other document and could not explain the difference from the P2.14 million total. Her extracted data covered 2022-2025; she could not confirm payments outside that period or whether the checks were deposited. She later expressly maintained that return of capital was not income.

02:14:56 · S0079402:16:38 · S0080504:50:24 · S0153404:52:11 · S42M_S01534_104:54:48 · S0155705:04:04 · S0161806:12:37 · S0200706:46:51 · S0225306:47:31 · S0225806:52:41 · S0231406:52:47 · S0231506:52:57 · S0231606:53:25 · S0231807:03:00 · S02373

She said the Vice President had no management or operational participation in GenCorp, including Times Square, and no authority to represent it in transactions or contracts. She identified Davao City and PhilHealth contracts, but the Chair later clarified with her that GenCorp itself bid for and received the awards, regardless of which branch supplied the food.

03:12:37 · S0084403:12:57 · S0084603:14:26 · S0085503:14:39 · S0085703:15:07 · S0085903:20:25 · S0089003:22:42 · S0090807:51:43 · S0268707:51:49 · S0268807:52:07 · S0268907:52:08 · S0269007:52:15 · S0269107:52:16 · S0269207:52:23 · S02693

For Metro City Chow, which operates Chowking's San Pedro branch in Davao City, she identified the Vice President as a 20% stockholder with P50,000 subscribed capital and P12,500 paid up, and as a listed director and compensation-committee member in the 2022-2025 information sheets. She said she had not seen the Vice President at Metro City Chow board meetings and that the compensation committee did not exist in its bylaws. On cross, she specified that she attended about two board meetings a year, could not rule out attendance at other meetings, and did not attend stockholders' meetings. She described the committee listing as an accounting-staff error and said the Vice President received no money from Metro City Chow.

03:26:47 · S0094103:27:16 · S0094503:28:33 · S0095703:29:12 · S42M_S00961_303:29:51 · S0096803:30:00 · S0096903:30:07 · S0097003:30:27 · S0097303:30:56 · S0097503:31:18 · S0097703:32:27 · S0098303:37:27 · S0101805:44:16 · S0180805:45:16 · S42M_S01815_205:45:27 · S42M_S01815_405:47:29 · S0183705:47:46 · S42M_S01838_305:46:30 · S0182306:49:18 · S0227906:49:20 · S02281

She acknowledged an error in GenCorp's beneficial-ownership declaration naming JTC Group of Companies rather than a natural person. She said she had advised an amendment, which was still undone, and that identifying the proper individuals required examining JTC's records. She acknowledged qualified audit opinions and read that Metro City Chow's auditors could not verify cash and observe inventory counting because of management-imposed limitations. She did not know those limitations, did not prepare the financial statements, and said she kept copies of information sheets after filing without independently reviewing their accuracy.
