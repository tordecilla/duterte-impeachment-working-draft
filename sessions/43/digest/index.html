Witnesses and evidence

Lawyer and defense witness Atty. Anel Martin S. Antero testified about a shared Tokyo dinner charged to Atty. Manases R. Carpio, the spouse of Vice President Sara Zimmerman Duterte. Defense counsel Atty. Lindon Miguel C. Bacquel offered the testimony to explain the ₱205,007.29 Ginza-Inakaya charge from May 19, 2023 and rebut Article 2 allegations.
