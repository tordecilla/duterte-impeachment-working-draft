Witnesses and evidence

Atty. Rainer P. Dela Fuente, a certified public accountant, lawyer and former AMLC financial investigator, testified for the defense on anti-money-laundering standards and financial-analysis methods. Rep. Jose Manuel “Chel” I. Diokno and senator-judges challenged the basis of his opinions because he had not reviewed the complete prosecution forensic report or its underlying case records. Dela Fuente cited confidentiality and his lack of authority to access the records, and described his answers as confined to displayed excerpts and hypothetical questions.

Dela Fuente described covered transaction reports (CTRs) and suspicious transaction reports (STRs) as indicators requiring verification, with bank inquiries supplying the underlying transaction records. He distinguished inflows from income, returned capital from earnings, and corporate receipts from shareholder income. On cross-examination, he agreed that a forensic accountant could use corporate-veil analysis in a professional report.

Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Sherwin T. Gatchalian contrasted ₱2.7 billion in bank transactions during 2009–2013 with the much smaller BIR income figures presented for Duterte, and asked whether the discrepancy warranted investigation, a freeze-order application or a red-flag assessment. He also asked what additional records were needed, raised bank amounts outside the SALNs, and asked whether the discrepancy was an impeachable offense. Defense witness and former AMLC financial investigator Dela Fuente said the figures merited further investigation and the normal freeze-order and bank-inquiry processes. He explained that covered persons flag suspicious activity and that investigators need source bank records to trace the money beyond CTR and STR snapshots; he deferred the question of impeachability to the impeachment court.

In a manifestation, Sen. Risa Hontiveros criticized the fairness of videos whose speakers were deceased or absent and could not be questioned. She later challenged Dela Fuente’s criticism without reading the full report or underlying records; he invoked confidentiality, defended comparing displayed passages with the law and affirmed respect for Cabrera’s qualifications. In her later round, Hontiveros examined Dela Fuente’s Cagayan Economic Zone Authority consultancy, casino-sector training and POGO or online-gambling clients.
