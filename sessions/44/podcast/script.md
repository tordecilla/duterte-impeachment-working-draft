## The witness and the central challenge

The defense presented Atty. Rainer P. Dela Fuente, a certified public accountant, lawyer and former Anti-Money Laundering Council financial investigator. His testimony on financial analysis raised a central challenge: he had not reviewed the complete prosecution forensic report or its underlying case records. Prosecutor Rep. Jose Manuel “Chel” I. Diokno and senator-judges questioned how far Dela Fuente’s opinions could go without that foundation. Dela Fuente cited confidentiality and his lack of authority to access the records. He said his answers addressed displayed excerpts and hypothetical questions, rather than a complete case analysis.

## Financial indicators and analytical methods

The witness distinguished financial signals from completed findings. Covered and suspicious transaction reports, he said, require verification; bank inquiries supply the underlying records. Inflows are not necessarily income, returned capital is not necessarily earnings, and corporate receipts are not automatically a shareholder's income. Vinluan challenged the attribution of corporate revenue, estimated franchise capital and insurance value to Duterte, as well as desktop research and calculations in the forensic report. On cross-examination, Dela Fuente accepted sensitivity analysis as an accounting method and corporate-veil analysis as professional work, while distinguishing that work from judicial action.
