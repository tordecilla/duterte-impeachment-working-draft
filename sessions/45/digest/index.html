Witnesses and evidence

Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Sherwin T. Gatchalian asked defense witness Dr. Ceferino S. Rodolfo, DTI Undersecretary and BOI Vice Chairman and Managing Head, why CALE88 Foods Corporation’s ₱208 million in audited sales for 2022–2025 differed from roughly ₱776 million in AMLC inward remittances, even adding ₱20 million in shareholder advances, and whether sales were understated. Rodolfo could find no explanation in the operational documents submitted to BOI. On the much smaller bank-certified receipts, Rodolfo totaled $721,283, approximately ₱41.83 million at ₱58 per dollar, and said BOI lacked bank certificates for 2024, when reported export sales reached ₱150 million. Gatchalian questioned why a BOI-registered exporter would forgo VAT zero-rating and other incentives, invoking the importance of VAT relief under CREATE MORE; Rodolfo did not know why the company had not applied and said exporters generally maximize available incentives. Gatchalian also questioned reliance on self-declared applications and operational reports. Rodolfo said these could be checked against audited financial statements and, for VAT zero-rating applications, bank certificates. Asked about bills of lading or other proof that goods actually left the country, Rodolfo acknowledged BOI did not require those customs documents and pointed to Bureau of Customs and Philippine Statistics Authority records. Gatchalian proposed examining customs documentation to verify actual exports.
