Dr. Ceferino S. Rodolfo

Defense witness; DTI Undersecretary; BOI Vice Chairman and Managing Head

He took the oath and testified before being excused by Presiding Officer Sen. Francis "Chiz" G. Escudero.

00:13:37 · S0002600:14:45 · S0003000:15:01 · S0200400:19:31 · S0006305:40:54 · S01546

Rodolfo testified that CALE88 Foods Corporation’s registration was issued on December 9, 2021 and that its reported commercial start was January 1, 2023. Its BOI Form S-1 submissions listed direct labor of 115 in 2023, 201 in 2024 and 50 in 2025. He said BOI had no recorded cancellation of registration or suspension of incentives.

01:07:02 · S0022601:26:56 · S0032401:07:18 · S0022701:27:03 · S0032504:48:04 · S0119903:28:46 · S0063103:28:53 · S0063203:29:10 · S00633

Rodolfo distinguished eligibility for incentives from actual use: CALE88 Foods Corporation used VAT zero rating in 2024 and 2026, covering 2023 and 2025 performance. It did not avail itself of an income-tax holiday; he said losses in its first two operating years made that holiday meaningless.

03:48:32 · S0076904:59:13 · S0127305:00:04 · S0127805:19:26 · S01385

Rodolfo said he had not personally visited CALE88 Foods Corporation’s plant and relied on company reports and financial statements. The 50-worker figure came from the notarized Form S-1 rather than a physical headcount. He said the request for a certificate of good standing and the reported fall in employment triggered a site visit.

03:43:49 · S0074503:43:54 · S0074603:44:24 · S0075203:44:36 · S0075404:36:30 · S0114604:37:36 · S01149

Rodolfo said BOI’s labor-compliance checks were tied to an income-tax-holiday application, while VAT zero-rating review concerned the export-to-sales ratio. BOI had not received the employee-benefit records raised on cross-examination. He also clarified that the ownership correspondence updated BOI records and noted the transfer, rather than approving it.

03:47:40 · S0076803:48:32 · S0076903:49:08 · S0077303:49:20 · S0077603:49:27 · S0301404:05:07 · S0090504:05:52 · S00911

Rodolfo put CALE88 Foods Corporation’s reported 2023–2025 sales at ₱208,748,899. When Sen. Sherwin T. Gatchalian compared this with about ₱776 million in AMLC inward remittances, Rodolfo said the BOI submissions supplied no explanation for receipts exceeding declared sales. He put bank-certified remittances at US$721,283 and said BOI lacked bank certificates for 2024, when the company reported approximately ₱150 million in exports.

02

Pilar Marilyn M. Pagayunan

Defense witness; Director IV, Center for Food Regulation and Research, FDA

She took the oath, testified and was excused by Presiding Officer Sen. Francis "Chiz" G. Escudero.

05:42:11 · S0156005:42:25 · S0156105:49:44 · S0159106:33:38 · S04135

Pagayunan described deficiencies in CALE88 Foods Corporation’s initial banana-chip application and its revised label. She said the revised label removed the cardaba claim and provided complete nutrition facts supported by a laboratory test result. She explained that a certificate of analysis supported the nutrition declaration.

05:55:28 · S0162006:02:42 · S0164406:03:34 · S0164606:05:36 · S01658

Prosecution counsel Atty. Reynaldo Robles stipulated that CALE88 Foods Corporation applied for two product registrations, for local manufacture and export, submitted appropriate requirements and received the certificates. Defense counsel Atty. Justin Nicol B. Gular added compliance with the requirements, which Robles accepted. Escudero later read this exchange back into the record.

06:11:04 · S0170806:11:38 · S0451706:11:44 · S0451806:34:09 · S01870

Pagayunan said her office evaluated product safety and quality and identified the FDA Field Regulatory Operations Office as the unit handling inspections. Escudero sustained an objection to further questioning on whether products were actually manufactured or sold and in what quantities, explaining that she could not answer those questions.
