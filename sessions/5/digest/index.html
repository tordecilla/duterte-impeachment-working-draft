At a glance

  • Bottom line: Two former LandBank branch managers testified about the procedures used for large OVP and DepEd cash encashments.
  • What the witnesses said: Both witnesses described advance notice, document checks, approvals, and cash release; Constantino said the encashment she discussed was not illegal, while Camposano distinguished unusual transactions from suspicious ones.
  • Senator-judge scrutiny: Senator-judges questioned the witnesses about the meaning of unusual transactions, AMLA reporting limits, bank safeguards, and the handling of large cash withdrawals.
  • Court action: The chair treated limited check-related stipulations as admissions for trial efficiency, ordered subpoenas for three later prosecution witnesses, and retained the original order of evidence.
  • What is next: The trial adjourned to August 3, when the prosecution was to continue presenting evidence on the non-withdrawn articles before the respondent's evidence in chief.
