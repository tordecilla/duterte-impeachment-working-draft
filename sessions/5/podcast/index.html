02

The big picture

The July 29 sitting heard two former LandBank branch managers, Violeta Constantino and Nenita Camposano, about high-value OVP and DepEd cash encashments. They described advance notice, verification, approvals, and cash release. Constantino said the encashment she discussed was not illegal, while Camposano distinguished unusual transactions from suspicious ones and declined to disclose STR information. Senator-judges pressed both witnesses on banking practice, AMLA confidentiality, and those labels. The court ordered subpoenas for later prosecution witnesses and retained the original evidence sequence.
