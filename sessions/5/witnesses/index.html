Nenita Camposano

Retired Land Bank of the Philippines department manager; prosecution witness

Camposano was directed to the witness stand and testified that the same process applied to the three checks: the DepEd Cash Division first called to reserve cash, with each check amounting to ₱37.5 million.

02:37:17 · S0076302:47:26 · S0080002:50:17 · S00824

03:22:05 · S01111
