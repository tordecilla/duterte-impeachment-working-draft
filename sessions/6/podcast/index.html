02

The big picture

NBI Director Melvin Matibag completed defense cross-examination and questioning by senator-judges. He described an unfinished inquiry that had narrowed its persons of interest but had not identified the person allegedly contracted to harm the President. The prosecution conducted no redirect, and the court excused him as its last Article Four witness.

Evidence boundaries

08

What remained unresolved

The alleged contractor remained unidentified, a possible conspiracy remained only an investigative angle, and confidential coordination reports were not in the impeachment-court record. Matibag said the inquiry was continuing and had not yet produced a case on the conspiracy theory.

What's next
