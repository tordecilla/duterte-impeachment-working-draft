04

Senator-judge interventions

Sen. Joel Villanueva first sought guidance on how material developments from the NBI's continuing investigation could be brought before the Impeachment Court after the Article 4 evidence closed. The Presiding Officer declined to resolve that hypothetical in advance, while recognizing that reopening could be available under the rules and leaving any move to the parties. Villanueva then asked Director Melvin Matibag how the NBI evaluates an allegation that someone ordered or contracted another person to commit a crime. He stressed that constitutional accountability in impeachment differs from criminal liability, and asked why an individual not yet identified is different from a conclusion that no such individual exists. Matibag distinguished the completed investigations into grave threats and inciting to sedition from the continuing effort to identify the alleged person contacted to kill.

04:02:53 · S0124604:06:27 · S0125304:10:14 · S01264

Sen. Raffy T. Tulfo used the warrantless arrest of teacher Ronell Mas as a comparison point after Director Melvin Matibag recounted that Mas had offered a PHP 50 million reward for the killing of former President Rodrigo Duterte. Tulfo challenged the contrast with Vice President Sara Duterte's case as special treatment, then asked whether Mayor Sebastian "Baste" Duterte's statement that President Marcos's head was the one they needed amounted to a bounty or threat, and why it had not led to an arrest. Matibag called the remark a threat to the President and national security, said he could not account for the earlier NBI treatment because he was not then with the bureau, and said Mas's case had been dismissed after the warrantless arrest, an outcome the NBI was guarding against.

04:12:05 · S0130404:13:18 · S0130904:13:28 · S0131004:14:05 · S0131204:15:12 · S0131504:15:39 · S0131704:16:34 · S0132504:16:42 · S01327

Sen. Risa Hontiveros sought the NBI's account of vlogger Princess Maui and of the competing accounts of Oplan Romanov. Director Melvin Matibag said he understood Princess Maui to be a DDS-aligned vlogger; she had been invited to the NBI but did not appear and remained under cybercrime monitoring. Hontiveros contrasted Atty. Lotoc's account of a threat from Mayor Sebastian "Baste" Duterte to the President and his family with Princess Maui's account of a threat to the Vice President and the Duterte family. Matibag said the earlier NBI inquiry had subpoenaed Princess Maui and the Vice President, neither of whom had apparently attended; he linked "Romanov" to Baste Duterte's January 2024 remarks and said a task force was continuing surveillance and investigation. Pressed on actionable intelligence, Matibag said the NBI was monitoring a continuing threat to the President but did not treat the assertion of a threat to the Vice President as actionable intelligence; he said that claim had come only from Princess Maui.

04:17:06 · S0133104:17:54 · S0133804:18:21 · S0134004:20:01 · S0134304:20:33 · S0134604:21:21 · S01348

Sen. Paolo Benigno "Bam" Aquino asked NBI Director Melvin Matibag for his professional assessment of whether Vice President Sara Duterte had actually spoken to someone about carrying out the killings she had mentioned, notwithstanding the continuing investigation. Matibag said the Vice President's own statement made him certain that she had; pressed for a probability, he answered 100 percent, saying she had described a plan and said the person contacted had agreed.

04:28:10 · S01421

Sen. Alan Peter Cayetano asked that his written continuing objection to the July 20 subpoena ruling be entered in the journal, rather than only in the record and transcript. He moved reconsideration of the previous day's journal approval so the objection could be included as an amendment.

04:29:23 · S01425

Questioning NBI Director Melvin Matibag, Cayetano said he accepted the need to investigate threats but challenged the task force's method of reading Vice President Sara Duterte's video. If it credited her statement that someone had agreed to kill three officials, he asked, why did it discount her preceding statement that she herself would be killed and her allegation of massive corruption? Matibag said the task force focused on the threat whose legal elements it found present and treated the other statement as cover for it. Cayetano then questioned Matibag's assertion that the NBI could investigate any crime motu proprio, citing Republic Act No. 10867's enumerated offenses and its provision for a presidential or Justice Department directive; Matibag said the list did not limit the bureau's role as the primary investigating body.

04:32:05 · S0143804:35:19 · S0147004:36:34 · S01472

Sen. Francis "Kiko" Pangilinan asked NBI Director Melvin Matibag whether a Vice President's higher trust rating than the President's could negate an allegation of betrayal of public trust. Matibag said no: surveys are a numerical snapshot, whereas betrayal of public trust is a constitutional impeachment ground measured against the evidence, not popularity. Pangilinan followed up to make that distinction clear for the public; Matibag agreed that either a positive or negative trust rating is irrelevant to a conviction or acquittal.

04:43:22 · S0151004:43:50 · S0151204:44:49 · S01515

Sen. Pia S. Cayetano challenged how the prosecution was using the Arturo Lascañas material. After lead prosecutor Gerville Luistro said it was offered to show what occurred in the NBI task-force investigation, Cayetano asked whether it could be authenticated: the document was not notarized, the witness had not seen it executed, and the defense said it bore no original signature. Luistro answered that the witness had not seen it executed and did not dispute the defense's point on the signature. Cayetano then turned to the rules on impeaching a witness through contradictory evidence or evidence bearing on truthfulness, but deferred the point when she could not locate her notes; the Chair reserved her first senator-judge intervention for the next sitting.

04:46:16 · S0153804:47:34 · S0155004:50:18 · S0157104:53:02 · S01585
